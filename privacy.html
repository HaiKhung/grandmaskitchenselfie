Privacy Policy

Grandma's Kitchen Photo – last updated 2026-11-05

What the app does

Grandma's Kitchen Photo lets you take a selfie with a Vietnamese conical hat, logo and frame added on top.

Camera

The app asks for permission to use your camera so it can show the live preview and take your photo. All processing, including face detection used to position the hat, happens on your device. Camera images and photos are not uploaded, stored on our servers or shared with anyone.

Your photos

A photo is only saved when you press Download or Share. You choose where it goes. We never receive a copy.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data. The app has no accounts, advertising or analytics.

Third-party content

The face-detection library is loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Like any website, jsDelivr can see your IP address when your device downloads that file. No photos or camera data are sent to it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
